1. Jollof Rice Recipe

Jollof rice is one of the most popular Nigerian dishes.It is prepared using rice,tomatoes and pepper,onions and spices.

The Following Ingredients Are Necessary In The Preparation Of Jollof Rice

  • 3 Cups of Rice
  • 5 Fresh Tomatoes
  • 2 red bell peppers (Tatashe)
  • 2 scotch bonnet peppers (Rodo)
  • 2 onions
  • Tomato paste
  • Vegetable oil
  • Chicken stock
  • Curry powder and thyme
  • Salt and seasoning cubes
  • Bay leaves (optional)

Preparation Steps

  1. Wash and blend the tomatoes, peppers and one onion until smooth.
  2. Heat vegetable oil in a pot and add chopped onions.
  3. Add tomato paste and fry for a few minutes.
  4. Pour in the blended pepper mixture and cook until the mixture thickens and the excess water reduces.
  5. Add curry, thyme, seasoning cubes, salt and chicken stock.
  6. Wash the rice and add it to the stew.
  7. Stir properly, cover the pot and cook on low heat until the rice is soft and has absorbed the
    8. sauce.
  8. Serve hot with fried plantain, chicken or beef.

2. Egusi Soup

Egusi is a food mostly eaten by those in the south and the east.

The Following Ingredients Are Used In The Preparation Of Egusi Soup

  • 2 Cups of groung egusi
  • Palm oil
  • Assorted Meat
  • Stockfish
  • Dryfish
  • Cray Fish
  • Pepper & onions
  • Seasoning cubes & salt
  • Ugu or spinach leaves
  • Locust beans(optional)

Preparation Steps

  1. Boil thee assorted meat and fish with onions,seasoning and salt until tender
  2. Mix the ground egusi with a little water to form a thick paste
  3. Hit little palm oil in a pot and add the egusi paste you mixed
  4. Stir the egusi until the colour has changd and till it looks like scrambled eggs
  5. In a clean pot,Pour your palm oil into the pot
  6. Heat the palm oil up a bit and add your locust beans and chopped onions,also add your crayfish
  7. Add your pepper and the stock from your assorted meat into the pepper
  8. Allow it fry wll,then you add your fried egusi into the fried pepper and add water to preference
  9. You taste and add seasoning and salt to your taste
  10. You add your washed ugu/spinach leaves,assorted meat,stock fish,dry fish and leave it for 10 - 15 mins and allow it cook
  11. Your egusi is ready and you can eat it with any food of your choice
